# B11 messaging: implementation plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-b11-messaging-design.md` (approved by Fabian 2026-10-07; anybody with a published profile or venue may start a conversation).

## Steps (test-first)

1. `MessagingIntegrationTest` (REST, limits, block, read state, booking thread, unread e-mail) and `MessagingWebSocketIntegrationTest` (real server, STOMP), failing.
2. Migration `V15__messaging.sql` (V14 is taken by L3 in PR #23): `conversation`, `conversation_message`, `conversation_read`; `notification_preference.message_email`.
3. Module `messaging`:
   - root: `Conversations` facade (unread count), `ConversationSide`, `MessageUnread` event;
   - `domain`: `Conversation` (DIRECT or BOOKING, artist + venue, block by side), `Message`, `ReadState` (read up to, reminded), `MessagingErrors`;
   - `application`: `ConversationService` (start, send, read, block, list, history, unread), `UnreadReminders` + `MessagingJob` (every minute), `BookingThreads` (listens to `BookingRequested`, implements booking's `BookingConversations`), `LivePush` (after commit, `/user/queue/messages`);
   - `infrastructure`: repositories, `WebSocketConfig` (STOMP `/ws`, simple broker, JWT on `CONNECT`, only `/user/queue/**` subscriptions);
   - `api`: `ConversationController`, `ConversationDtos`.
4. `booking`: `BookingConversations` interface in the root, `conversationId` on the booking response (no dependency on messaging).
5. `venue`: `Venues.findPublishedBySlug`.
6. `notification`: `MessageUnreadMailer` on `MailLayout`, `messages.email` in preferences.
7. `/ws/**` open in `SecurityConfig` (the STOMP `CONNECT` carries the token).
8. Error and mail texts in `messages_pl/en.properties`; `./gradlew test` (incl. `ModularityTest`), `pnpm api:generate`, CLAUDE.md bullet.

## Defaults taken while planning

- Read state is per person; messages of the reader's own side never count as unread. "Read" for the other side = any of its people read it (artist, or anybody in the venue team).
- The unread e-mail goes only while the conversation's latest message is from the other side (nobody on the reader's side answered yet), so a venue team is not mailed about a message a teammate already answered.
- While a direct conversation is blocked nobody writes in it, the blocking side included (it unblocks first); only the side that blocked can unblock.
- Booking threads without messages stay out of the list; they open from the booking.
- Both limits answer 422 (`MESSAGING_LIMIT`, `MESSAGING_RATE`) until B16 maps 429.
- The unread counter for the menu counts conversations with unread messages (and also returns the number of messages).
- The e-mail does not quote the message, only who wrote and a link to `WEB_BASE_URL/messages/{id}`.
